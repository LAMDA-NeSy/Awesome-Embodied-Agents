## Training Target and Interface

PEAR post-trains Qwen3.8-27B with robot-use reinforcement learning in LIBERO and RoboDojo. The reasoning model itself serves as the policy. Camera frames and arm state are observations; responses contain reasoning and JSON end-effector pose deltas. Inverse kinematics and a robot controller execute the actions.

Task completion supplies a binary episode reward. Reasoning and action tokens receive learning signals from the episode outcome. Training uses no demonstrations. See Section 3 and Figure 2 for the interaction protocol, and the official repository for training infrastructure.
